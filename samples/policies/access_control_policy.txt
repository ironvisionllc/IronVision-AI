IronVision Demo Agency – Access Control Policy (SYNTHETIC)

1. Purpose
This policy establishes access control requirements for agency information systems in accordance with NIST SP 800-53 Rev 4.

2. Scope
Applies to all agency systems, including the Logistics Tracking Platform (LTP). All content is synthetic demo data.

3. Roles
The policy is maintained by the Office of the CISO. Approving official: TBD

4. Account Management
Accounts that remain inactive are disabled after 35 days of inactivity. Account reviews occur quarterly.

5. Unsuccessful Logon Attempts
Systems lock the account after 3 consecutive invalid logon attempts within 15 minutes.

6. Session Lock
A session lock is initiated after 15 minutes of inactivity and is retained until the user re-authenticates.

7. Review
This policy is reviewed annually.
